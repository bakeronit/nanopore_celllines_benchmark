Genome stratification of FP and FN
================

Here, we study the genomic location of FP and FN calls with the genome
stratification from GIAB
[v3.4](https://ftp-trace.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ReferenceSamples/giab/release/genome-stratifications/v3.4/).
This analysis is limited on chromosome 1-22.
